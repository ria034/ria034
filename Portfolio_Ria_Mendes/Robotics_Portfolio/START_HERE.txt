ROBOTICS PORTFOLIO — HOW TO OPEN

1. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted Robotics_Portfolio folder.
3. Double-click index.html.
4. Click a project card or its Explore link to open its detail page.

IMPORTANT: Do NOT open index.html while browsing inside the ZIP in Windows Explorer.
That creates a temporary path and relative project links can fail.

Included project pages: prism.html, glimmer.html, trace.html, mpc.html, rl-d-lite.html.
All pages must remain together in the same extracted folder.
